LAST LIGHT: CASTLEFALL

Privacy & Support

Effective 7 November 2026. This page covers the Last Light: Castlefall app for iPhone and iPad.

Privacy policySupportContact

In short

  • There is no account and no sign-up. Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The developer does not collect, receive or sell any personal information.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device data to show and measure ads, and asks your permission where the law or iOS requires it.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details.

Privacy policy

Who we are

Last Light: Castlefall is made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, see Contact below.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves your kingdoms, Legacy, achievements, settings and progress in the app’s storage on your device. This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us, and we have no servers that receive it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not run our own analytics, we do not ask for your name, email or location, and we do not have any user accounts.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows an occasional full-screen ad at dawn between nights, provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device’s advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be inferred;
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language;
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information about how ads perform.

Before the first ad is shown, iOS asks whether the app may track you across apps and websites. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, ads are still shown but are not personalised using that identifier. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchase (Remove Ads)

Remove Ads is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment. We only receive confirmation that the purchase was made, so the game can turn ads off. Use “Restore Purchases” under Options in the game to restore it on a new device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for teenagers.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data collected by Google for advertising, use the options above or Google’s own tools. You can remove all game data from your device by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling data differently, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top.

Support

I lost my progress

Progress is saved on your device after every day and dawn. If the app is closed during a night, you return to that morning when you open it again. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and progress does not move between devices.

Remove Ads didn’t carry over to my new device

On the title screen, open Options and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

There is no sound

Check the volume buttons and the in-game sound settings under Options. The game plays its sound even with the ring/silent switch on.

Something else is wrong

Send a message to the address below with your device model and what happened, and a screenshot if you can.
