Bloomstead — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Bloomstead ("the game") is made by an independent developer. This policy explains what data is processed when you play.

Data we store

Your game progress (levels, scores, currency, settings) is stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not collect your name, email, or contacts.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. iOS asks for your permission before any cross-app tracking; you can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See Google's advertising policy.

Purchases (Apple & RevenueCat)

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases; it receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts, never your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all locally stored game data. For questions, contact the support email listed on the App Store page.
